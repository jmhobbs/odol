P3D Converter

Converts an DayZ ODOL or MLOD .p3d file to MLOD + model.cfg or FBX. FBX files will have all proxies removed, and will be the highest detail LOD in the P3D.

Everything runs locally in your browser - your file is never uploaded.

Preview of the converted FBX geometry. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.

Also available as a command line tool, github.com/jmhobbs/odol
