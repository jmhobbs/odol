FBX to P3D

Build a basic DayZ model from FBX files. Geometry, View Geometry, Fire Geometry, and Memory LODs are added from the bounding box of all graphical LODs, and every graphical LOD gets a camoground named selection. This isn't meant to be a full-featured P3D editor, it's just a less painful way to import your FBX files into DayZ.

Everything runs locally in your browser - your files are never uploaded. To convert an existing .p3d instead, use the P3D converter.

Graphical LODs

Order rows from most to least detailed. A row's resolution is its position: the first row is resolution 1, the next is 2, and so on.
